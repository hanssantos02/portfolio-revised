# Design System: Hans Santos

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Editorial engineering studio"**

Hans approved this direction after reviewing three alternatives. Large sans-serif type and generous space explain the offer; authentic project imagery supplies the detail. The design should feel personal, clear, and deliberate.

## Colors

Warm ivory surfaces, near-black readable text, and one controlled cobalt accent. Exact implemented tokens will be captured after the mockup and build. The orange-accent mix was not selected.

## Typography

Large sans-serif headings with an approachable, clear voice. Smaller body text remains readable and has comfortable line lengths. The final obtainable face and measured sizes follow the visual mockup.

## Layout

Generous space, service rows, and substantial project previews. Keep the approved sequence: introduction, services, selected work, working approach, contact. Collapse fluidly to a single column on narrow screens.

## Elevation & Depth

Quiet, flat surfaces. Project images carry visual complexity; avoid competing decorative effects.

## Shapes

Simple functional geometry that supports the type and imagery. Resolve exact corners and controls in the mockup, then document the implemented values.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Use authentic web project screenshots and labeled illustrative hardware diagrams.
- Keep keyboard focus visible and meaningful text visible without animation.
- Respect reduced motion.
- Do not invent project photography, clients, results, credentials, or testimonials.
- Do not reintroduce dark or orange styling as a competing direction.
